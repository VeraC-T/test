Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about working together

What types of projects do you work on?

I specialize in digital art commissions, brand illustrations, gallery exhibitions, and collaborative art projects. I'm particularly passionate about projects that explore the intersection of technology and creativity.

What's your typical project timeline?

Project timelines vary depending on complexity and scope. Simple commissions typically take 2-4 weeks, while larger projects can take 2-6 months. I'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes, I include up to 3 rounds of revisions in all my projects to ensure the final result meets your vision. Additional revisions can be discussed if needed.

Can you work with tight deadlines?

I can accommodate urgent projects depending on my current workload. Rush projects may include an additional fee and I'll let you know upfront if the timeline is feasible.
